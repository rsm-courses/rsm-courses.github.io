# Self-guided materials

Lesson materials you can work through on your own, outside of a scheduled
course. Organised by topic; each links out to the relevant lesson pages or
external Carpentry lesson site.

- _Nothing published yet — add entries here as materials become available._

## Adding new materials

Materials can live directly in this `materials/` folder as markdown pages, or
link out to external lesson sites (e.g. a Carpentries lesson repo). Keep each
entry short: a title, one-line description, and a link.
